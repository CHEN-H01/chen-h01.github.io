## About Me

I am a Third-year PhD student at [The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK)](https://www.cuhk.edu.hk) supervised by [Prof. Pheng-Ann Heng](https://appsrv.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~pheng/index.php) and [Prof. Chi-Wing Fu](https://www.cse.cuhk.edu.hk/~cwfu/). 

My research aims to develop robotic foundation models for intelligent robots capable of understanding, reasoning, and acting in the physical world. My current research interests include <span style="color:#39c">Vision-Language-Action Models, World-Action Models, and Multimodal Learning</span>.

Feel free to reach out - I'm always open to discussions and potential collaborations! 😄
